PMS4Man Support

PMS4Man helps people who support a woman: simple cycle estimates and supportive suggestions for each phase.

FAQ

The dates are off. What should I do?
Record in the app when a period starts (“Started today” or “Log another date”). With records, estimates use the average of real cycles.

My Apple Watch shows no one.
Open the app on your iPhone once: it sends data to the watch whenever it opens or something changes.

Reminders don't arrive.
Check that reminders are on in the app (bell icon) and that notifications for PMS4Man are allowed in iPhone Settings.

Where is my data stored?
Only on your device. See the privacy policy.
