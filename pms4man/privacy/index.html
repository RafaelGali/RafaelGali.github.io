PMS4Man Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026.

Summary: PMS4Man does not collect, send or share any data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

The names of the people you follow, the period start dates you record, cycle length, the photos you choose for avatars and your reminder preferences. This data stays only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No data sent to servers, the cloud or third parties.
  • No analytics, tracking or ads.
  • No access to your photo library: photos are chosen with the system picker, and the app only receives the image you pick.

Apple Watch, widgets and reminders

The Apple Watch app receives a summary directly from your iPhone, over the connection between the two devices, without any server. Widgets and complications show only initials, and their content is hidden while the device is locked. Reminders, when you turn them on, are created on your iPhone.

Deleting your data

Delete the person in the app or uninstall the app. Since there is no copy on any server, the data is gone.

Estimates

The phases and dates shown are estimates for regular cycles and are for reference only. PMS4Man does not provide diagnosis or medical advice.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the support page. If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
