PMS4Man Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026.

Summary: PMS4Man does not collect or share any data. Everything you enter stays on your devices and in your iCloud, and we can't see it.

What the app stores

The names of the women you follow, the period start dates you record, cycle length, the photos you choose for avatars and your reminder preferences. This data stays on your devices, in the app's own storage, and is synced between them through your iCloud, in the private database of your Apple Account. Only you can access it: we never see or receive anything.

iCloud

Sync uses your own iCloud, protected by Apple. To turn it off, open Settings > [your name] > iCloud and switch off PMS4Man; your data stays on the device.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No data sent to our own servers or to third parties: the only copy outside your devices is in your iCloud.
  • No analytics, tracking or ads.
  • No access to your photo library: photos are chosen with the system picker, and the app only receives the image you pick.

Apple Watch, widgets and reminders

The Apple Watch app receives a summary directly from your iPhone, over the connection between the two devices, without any server. Widgets and complications show a photo or initials, never her name; you can hide the photo in each place in Settings, and their content is hidden while the device is locked. Reminders, when you turn them on, are created on your iPhone.

Deleting your data

Delete her profile in the app: it is also removed from your iCloud and your other devices. Uninstalling the app removes only that device's copy; to remove everything from iCloud, use Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Estimates

The phases and dates shown are estimates for regular cycles and are for reference only. PMS4Man does not provide diagnosis or medical advice.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the support page. If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
